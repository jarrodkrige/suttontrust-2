Here are **five UK‑specific statistics** about **first‑generation (first‑in‑family) university students**, each with a **source and year**. I’ve avoided inventing figures; where interpretation is broad, I flag limits.

***

### 1) Around **two‑thirds of UK graduates are first‑generation**

*   **Stat:** Approximately **66% of UK graduates** are the first in their family to attend university.
*   **Source:** UCAS / FirstGens, citing UK research aggregated from higher‑education datasets
*   **Year:** Statement used by UCAS and FirstGens pages current in **2023–2024**
*   **Notes:** This is a widely cited headline figure, but UCAS does not publish a single technical paper behind it; it is best treated as an **approximate national estimate**, not a precise census count. [\[ucas.com\]](https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/students-with-individual-needs/first-generation-students), [\[firstgens.co.uk\]](https://firstgens.co.uk/)

***

### 2) **Two‑thirds of young degree‑holders are “first in family”**

*   **Stat:** Among a nationally representative English cohort born in **1989–90**, **about two‑thirds of those who gained a degree** were the **first in their family** to do so.
*   **Source:** Nuffield‑funded research using the *Next Steps* longitudinal study (UCL Centre for Longitudinal Studies)
*   **Year:** **2020** (analysis published August 2020; data collected up to 2015–16) [\[nuffieldfo...dation.org\]](https://www.nuffieldfoundation.org/news/first-in-family-university-students-need-more-guidance-navigating-education-system)

***

### 3) First‑generation students are **more likely to drop out**

*   **Stat:** Degree completion rates were **around 4 percentage points lower** for first‑generation students compared with those with graduate parents.
*   **Source:** Nuffield Foundation / Oxford Review of Education (UCL CLS, *Next Steps* cohort)
*   **Year:** **2020** [\[nuffieldfo...dation.org\]](https://www.nuffieldfoundation.org/news/first-in-family-university-students-need-more-guidance-navigating-education-system)

***

### 4) First‑generation students are **less likely to attend Russell Group universities**

*   **Stat:** First‑in‑family students were **significantly less likely** to attend a **Russell Group** institution than students with graduate parents, even after accounting for attainment and background.
*   **Source:** UCL Centre for Longitudinal Studies, funded by the Nuffield Foundation
*   **Year:** **2020** [\[nuffieldfo...dation.org\]](https://www.nuffieldfoundation.org/news/first-in-family-university-students-need-more-guidance-navigating-education-system)
*   **Notes:** The study reports direction and magnitude clearly, but does **not provide one single headline percentage** suitable for quoting without caveats.

***

### 5) About **two‑thirds of UK graduates are first‑generation**, higher among women

*   **Stat:** **Roughly two‑thirds of UK graduates** are first‑generation, with the share **higher among women than men**.
*   **Source:** UCL Social Research Institute, using the **1970 British Cohort Study**
*   **Year:** **2025** (UCL News release, data followed individuals to age 46) [\[ucl.ac.uk\]](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/news/2025/sep/first-generation-female-graduates-more-likely-not-have-children-their-40s-peers)
*   **Notes:** This reinforces earlier findings and confirms the scale of first‑generation participation among graduates.

***

## Quick caveat on definitions

Across UK data, *“first‑generation”* / *“first‑in‑family”* usually means **neither parent has a higher‑education qualification**, but definitions vary slightly by dataset and self‑reporting method (as noted by the Office for Students). [\[officefors...nts.org.uk\]](https://www.officeforstudents.org.uk/for-providers/equality-of-opportunity/equality-of-opportunity-risk-register-eorr/student-characteristics/first-in-family/)
