> Team 2 · Role copywriter-2 · 2026-05-08 12:54

University can change your life because it stretches you in ways you didn’t even know you needed. You start seeing new ideas, new people, and new versions of yourself, not all at once, but gradually, through conversations, challenges, and little moments that stick. It’s not just about a degree; it’s about realising you belong in spaces you might never have pictured yourself in before.
It also gives you confidence that comes from figuring things out on your own. You learn how to ask questions, make mistakes, and keep going anyway, skills that don’t disappear after graduation. Even when it’s hard, university can open doors you didn’t know existed and help you believe that your future can be bigger than where you started.
