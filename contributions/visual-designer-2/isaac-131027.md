Apply colour to a university marketing website using the following palette and intent. Base the UI on a soft blue and light orange theme that feels calm, modern, and quietly optimistic, suitable for sixth‑formers and first‑generation students. Use Soft Campus Blue (#4E6F8A) for primary actions, navigation, and key headings; Light Sky Blue (#8FAFBE) for panels, section backgrounds, and secondary elements; Warm Light Orange (#E6A15C) sparingly for highlights, CTAs, and moments of emphasis. Use Off‑White (#F4F1EC) as the main background, Stone Grey (#C9C4BC) for dividers and secondary UI, and Deep Ink Blue (#2E3F4B) for body text and accessibility contrast. Avoid high saturation, gradients, or corporate gloss. Colour usage should feel honest, welcoming, and grounded, guiding attention gently rather than shouting.
